<objective>
Add `main.py --mode certify_rs` backed by a new `util/certify_rs_eval.py` (mirroring the
`util/defense_compare.py` run_* convention). It: (1) runs a timing smoke test on 5-10 samples
and extrapolates wall-clock BEFORE the full sweep (locked decision #2); (2) certifies the
RML2016.10a test set per (SNR, modulation) cell, capped ~50/cell, at SNR points {-6, 0, 6, 12, 18},
for each noise-augmented checkpoint in the sigma grid {0.002, 0.005, 0.01, 0.02}, using Cohen
params n0=100, n=10000, alpha=0.001; (3) emits a certified-accuracy-vs-L2-radius CSV + curve;
and (4) runs the matched CW comparison (locked decision #3): measure each CW perturbation's
ACHIEVED per-sample raw-IQ L2 norm, bucket by that norm, and compare CW empirical accuracy per
bucket against RS certified accuracy at the corresponding radius. RS is framed as the L2-certified
baseline; L∞/L1 attacks (FGSM/PGD/EAD) stay empirical-only.

Purpose: RS-03 produces the certified-accuracy-vs-radius CSVs Phase 6 consumes for camera-ready
Table I. It reuses SmoothedClassifier (RS-01) and the noise checkpoints (RS-02).
Output: `util/certify_rs_eval.py`, `main.py` dispatch, and CSV/figure artifacts under inference/.
</objective>
