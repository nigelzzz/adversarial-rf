## Cross-Phase Integration

All three phases wire together end-to-end as a single reproducible pipeline:

1. **Phase 1 → Phase 2:** `util/defense_registry.py` exposes all 9 defenses via
   `DEFENSE_REGISTRY`; `util/defense_compare.py` in Phase 2 iterates over that
   registry against 5 attacks × 10 SNRs and writes CSVs.
2. **Phase 2 → Phase 3:** `paper/figures/generate_figures.py` reads the CSVs
   under `inference/2016.10a_165/result/defense_compare/` and produces the
   11 figures referenced by the 7 section files in `paper/latex/sections/`.
3. **E2E reproducibility:** `paper/reproduce.sh` replays the whole chain
   (calibration → defense_compare → figures → latexmk) from one entry point.

No cross-phase breaks.
